🇮🇳 How to Adapt Your Pitch for Indian Interview Styles

Tailor your depth and emphasis depending on whether you are interviewing with a Service IT giant, a Big 4 / Analytics consultancy, or a Product / Mature BI engineering team.

Tier 1: Service-Based IT TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, LTIMindtree

Coding Accuracy & Core Troubleshooting

  • DAX Live Coding: Expect screen-share tests on CALCULATE, ALL vs ALLSELECTED, RANKX, and Time Intelligence.
  • SQL Window Functions: Top N per department, deduplicating GST invoices with ROW_NUMBER(), running totals.
  • Power Query & Service: Merge vs Append row explosion, Gateway credential failures, and Dynamic vs Static RLS setups.
Tier 2: Consulting & Analytics Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC, Tiger, MuSigma, Fractal

Business Framing, Reconciliation & Trade-offs

  • Stakeholder Translation: Why you chose specific KPIs (ROAS, CPA, Margin Bridge) and how you structured Executive vs Drill-through views.
  • End-to-End Ownership: Combining upstream Alteryx/SQL prep with downstream Power BI modeling.
  • Number Reconciliation: How you proved to a Finance VP or Marketing Director that Power BI matched source ERP/Redshift down to the rupee.
Tier 3: Product & Mature BI GCCs, FinTech, SaaS, E-Commerce

Engine Internals, Scale & CI/CD Governance

  • VertiPaq & Profiling: Formula Engine vs Storage Engine, dictionary/RLE compression, cardinality reduction, DAX Studio Server Timings.
  • Enterprise Architecture: Composite models, Direct Lake in Microsoft Fabric, aggregations on 25M+ row tables, Redshift/Azure integration.
  • BI Engineering: Tabular Editor Calculation Groups, ALM Toolkit schema diffs, Deployment Pipelines, and Git version control.
